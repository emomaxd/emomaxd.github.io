I’m Emre. I work on Mesa and Linux graphics.

I want my work to become part of something larger than me. That is what I like about free software.

now

I’ve been contributing to Mesa since summer 2025.

My focus is Jay and Xe3, alongside BRW and ANV.

Jay is Intel’s new SSA-based shader compiler for Xe2 and Xe3.

I follow Intel Xe kernel development on the dri-devel mailing list.

I’m looking for GPU compiler or driver work.

systems background

I upstreamed the initial NuttX port for RK3588S and Orange Pi 5 Pro.

At Baykar, I worked on the NuttX port for TI AM67A.

background

I started programming in 2017.

lore

From 2019 to 2022, I made Unity games alone and with friends, then moved to OpenGL, Vulkan, operating systems, and compilers.
