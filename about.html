I’m Emre. I work on Mesa and Linux graphics.

I want my work to become part of something larger than me. That is what I like about free software.

now

I’ve been contributing to Mesa since summer 2025, mostly around GPU compilers and drivers.

Lately, most of that has been around Jay, BRW, and ANV.

I’m looking for GPU compiler or graphics driver work.

systems background

I upstreamed the initial NuttX port for RK3588S and Orange Pi 5 Pro.

At Baykar, I worked on the TI AM67A NuttX port for the T3 Gemstone project.

background

I started programming in 2017.

lore

Even before I started programming, I grew up around game modding, Minecraft servers, rooted phones, and custom ROMs. I was always curious about what was happening underneath, and that eventually pulled me toward hardware, operating systems, compilers, and microarchitecture.

From 2019 to 2022, I made Unity games alone and with friends, then moved to OpenGL and Vulkan. Graphics was where a lot of things came together for me: GPUs, performance, low-level programming, and the way software maps onto hardware. That is what eventually led me toward graphics drivers and GPU compilers.
