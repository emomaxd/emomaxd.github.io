# Third-party artwork

- `images/mesa-logo.svg` is derived from the Mesa logo at
  <https://mesa3d.org/assets/logo.svg>; its background rectangle was removed
  for transparent display.
- `images/llvm-dragon-legacy.png` is LLVM's older dragon logo from
  <https://llvm.org/Logo.html>. Apple Inc. retains its copyright and makes it
  available for royalty-free use under the terms published on that page.
- `images/u-boot-logo.svg` is the U-Boot logo from
  <https://docs.u-boot.org/en/latest/learn/logo.html>, copyright Heinrich
  Schuchardt and licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0.
- `images/nuttx-logo.png` is a cropped and resized rendering of NuttX's
  simplified logo from <https://nuttx.apache.org/docs/latest/logos/index.html>,
  created by Alan C. Assis and licensed under CC BY 4.0.
- `images/tux.png` is a resized rendering of the CC0 vector from
  <https://github.com/garrett/Tux>. Tux was originally created by Larry Ewing
  using GIMP; the vector was redrawn by Garrett LeSage and refined by IFo
  Hancroft.
